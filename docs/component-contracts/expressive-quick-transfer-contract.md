# Expressive Quick Transfer Contract

Purpose:
- Quick transfer supports the central payment owner and gives users a fast money-movement entry point.

Contract:
- The whole row is the CTA.
- Copy stays limited to title and last recipient.
- The visual language uses the payment primary role and a single leading transfer mark.
- The row may use a subtle primary-container surface to distinguish money movement from passive status modules.
- Press feedback is tactile and restrained.

Approved copy:
- `تحويل سريع`
- `آخر مستفيد: شركة الكهرباء`

Rejected:
- Detached text-button CTAs.
- Yellow/green fills unless the bank profile explicitly owns those roles.
- Extra explanatory text.
