# Prism Surface Contract

Prism surface roles:
- `prismBackground`: page foundation.
- `prismSurface`: base content surface.
- `prismSurfaceRaised`: cards with mild depth.
- `prismSurfaceFloating`: nav, sheets, and payment control surfaces.
- `prismSurfaceGlass`: translucent overlays.
- `prismSurfaceMuted`: quiet secondary surfaces.
- `prismSurfaceStrong`: focal containment.

Use spacing and hierarchy before adding surface weight. Not every row needs a container.
