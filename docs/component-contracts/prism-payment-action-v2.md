# Prism Payment Action V2

`SignalPrismPaymentAction` is the primary payment control.

Target:
- 68-72dp control.
- 18-24dp perceived protrusion above the dock.
- 28-30dp icon.
- One controlled Prism payment gradient.
- No blob, puddle, thick ring, idle pulse, or heavy glow.

The action should feel iconic but not compete with the account card.

Interaction:
- Soft press scale.
- Restrained shadow.
- Future haptic hook belongs in Signal feedback contracts.
