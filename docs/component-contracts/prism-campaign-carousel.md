# Prism Campaign Carousel

`SignalPrismCampaignCarousel` is premium bank communication, not an ad banner.

Contract fields:
- `id`
- `eyebrow`
- `title`
- `message`
- `actionLabel`
- `priority`
- `expiresAt`
- `deeplink`

Rules:
- Campaign appears after core daily money context.
- Visual energy is allowed, but it must stay below account hero and payment action.
- Future backend can provide image, gradient, priority, expiry, and route.
