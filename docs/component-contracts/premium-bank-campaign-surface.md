# Premium Bank Campaign Surface

Purpose:
- Campaigns are banking communication, not ads.

Contract:
- One campaign surface on Home.
- Neutral surface first, with sparse accent for label/icon/CTA.
- Copy must explain financial relevance.
- The surface should support future backend image/campaign content without requiring decoration now.

Current Home copy:
- `عرض مؤهل`
- `تقسيط مشترياتك الكبيرة`
- `يمكنك تحويل حركات البطاقة المؤهلة إلى أقساط.`
- `عرض التفاصيل`

Rejected:
- Banner advertising language.
- Loud campaign colors.
- Decorative gradients or stock-like visuals.
