# Promotional Surface Contract

Promotional surfaces are premium banking communication, not ads.

Rules:
- Maximum one Home promotion.
- Neutral surface first; accent only for labels or CTA text.
- Copy must explain financial relevance.
- No banner shouting, decorative gradients, or campaign clutter.

Approved Home copy:
- Label: `عرض مؤهل`
- Title: `تقسيط مشترياتك الكبيرة`
- Message: `يمكنك تحويل حركات البطاقة المؤهلة إلى أقساط.`
- CTA: `عرض التفاصيل`
