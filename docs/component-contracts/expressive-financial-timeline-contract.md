# Expressive Financial Timeline Contract

Purpose:
- Home timeline previews the user's financial life without becoming Notification Center.

Contract:
- Show three Home items only.
- The first item is allowed stronger containment when action is required.
- Type indicators must be meaningful icons derived from event category or tone.
- Rows remain connected by rhythm and spacing, not by a giant pale feed card.
- No random dots and no repeated chevrons.
- Full timeline history belongs outside Home.

Current demo:
- OpenWave approval, urgent now.
- Incoming LyPay transfer.
- USD rate alert.

RTL:
- Time/status remains visually paired to the row edge.
- Event title and metadata stay Arabic-first while Latin tokens remain readable.
