# Prism Card Personality Styles

Card styles map to Prism personalities:
- Classic Bank: restrained navy, minimal gradient.
- Premium Wallet: deep navy with cyan/violet.
- Youth Fintech: brighter controlled accent.
- Corporate Dense: high-contrast dark, low ornament.
- Islamic Calm: emerald/teal restraint.
- Merchant Energy: teal/cyan payment-forward.

Styles must never reduce PAN, expiry, status, or action readability.
