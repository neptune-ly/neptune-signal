# Prism Sample Themes

Date: 2026-05-17

## Purpose

These sample theme notes describe how Prism personalities should exercise the current design rules without adding Kotlin in this documentation-only pass.

## Shared Rules

All sample themes must follow the same gradient policy:

- gradients only for hero/account/card artwork,
- gradients only for transfer ritual fields,
- gradients only for campaign media overlays,
- nav/actions/cards/lists/settings use flat or tonal surfaces.

## Premium Wallet

- Personality: `PremiumWallet`
- Nav style: `FloatingDock`
- Gradient intensity: controlled
- Best use: flagship consumer wallet experience
- Notes: keep account/card artwork expressive, but keep Home, notifications, and service rows tonal.

## Classic Bank

- Personality: `ClassicBank`
- Nav style: `ClassicDock`
- Gradient intensity: minimal
- Best use: traditional retail bank
- Notes: prefer calm surfaces, conservative motion, and subtle account artwork.

## Corporate Dense

- Personality: `CorporateDense`
- Nav style: `DenseOperational`
- Gradient intensity: minimal
- Best use: business banking and operations
- Notes: dense lists, low motion, flat controls, no decorative campaign treatment in utility flows.

## Islamic Calm

- Personality: `IslamicCalm`
- Nav style: default Prism unless bank policy overrides
- Gradient intensity: controlled
- Best use: restrained emerald/teal influence
- Notes: use calm brand statement account artwork, not broad page gradients.

## Merchant Energy

- Personality: `MerchantEnergy`
- Nav style: `PaymentForward`
- Gradient intensity: expressive
- Best use: merchant and payment-forward apps
- Notes: payment can be emphasized through solid payment tone and ritual visuals; persistent nav remains tonal.

## Youth Fintech

- Personality: `YouthFintech`
- Nav style: default Prism unless policy overrides
- Gradient intensity: expressive
- Best use: energetic consumer banking
- Notes: expression belongs in account/card artwork, campaigns, and ritual motion. Operational surfaces still stay tonal.

## Review Checklist

- Theme does not override navigation with gradients.
- Payment action remains solid tone.
- Campaign samples use media overlay policy.
- Account/card artwork has contrast protection.
- OLED reduces glow and avoids muddy color blends.
