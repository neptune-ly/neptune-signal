# Foundations: Iconography

Icons replace words when the action is familiar and safe:

- Copy.
- Share.
- Refresh.
- Back.
- Close.
- Search.
- Filter.
- Download.
- Visibility mask.

## Rules

- Icon-only buttons require accessible labels.
- Minimum touch target: `44 x 44`.
- Icons inside boxes must be visually centered.
- Directional icons must flip in RTL.
- Accent-colored icons must communicate a meaningful state, not random decoration.
- No emoji in product UI.
