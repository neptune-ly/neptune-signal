# Accessibility Contract

Signal Prism must preserve accessibility while allowing premium visuals.

## Requirements

- Touch targets should remain at least 44-48dp.
- Text over gradients needs protective surfaces or overlays.
- Reduced motion must affect animation work.
- Reduced feedback must silence nonessential sound and haptics.
- Copy/reveal controls must have explicit labels.
- Disabled and locked states must be visible and announced.
- Destructive actions must be visually and semantically distinct.

## Component Requirements

- Nav items expose labels.
- Campaign CTAs expose purpose.
- Notification rows announce unread/action-required state.
- Card reveal announces temporary reveal and remask.
- Copy actions announce what was copied without exposing sensitive values.
