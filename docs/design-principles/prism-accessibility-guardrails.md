# Prism Accessibility Guardrails

Prism is richer than strict Material, but it must remain financially safe and accessible.

Guardrails:
- Tap targets stay at least 44-48dp.
- Small text never sits directly on unprotected gradients.
- Financial numbers use stable alignment and strong contrast.
- OLED reduces glow and gradient intensity.
- Reduced motion disables non-essential sheen, pulse, and carousel motion.
- Semantic colors remain meaningful: success, warning, danger, payment, brand.
- RTL is designed natively, not mirrored afterward.

Material remains a reference for accessibility, navigation semantics, and motion usability.
