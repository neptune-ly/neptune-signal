# Material 3 Expressive Strategy

Status: Active

## Interpretation

Material 3 Expressive is not a license to add more color or effects. The Google research describes expressive design through color, shape, size, motion, and containment used to help users notice what matters and navigate faster.

For Neptune. Signal, expressive means:

- key banking actions are easier to find,
- financial hierarchy is obvious,
- grouped content is easier to scan,
- motion reinforces continuity,
- touch targets feel confident,
- emotional tone is calm and trustworthy.

## Neptune Rules

### Color

Use color as attention guidance. Do not use color as decoration.

- Navy: trust and financial surfaces.
- Teal: primary money movement.
- Coral: risk, warning, signature moments.
- Neutral surfaces: most app structure.

### Shape

Shape expresses containment and priority.

- Primary account/card surfaces may be larger and more distinctive.
- Root nav surfaces stay stable and restrained.
- Avoid over-rounded toy surfaces.

### Size

Size creates hierarchy.

- Balance/account hero is the largest financial object.
- Center payment action is prominent but not louder than the account hero.
- Secondary actions stay visually quieter.

### Motion

Motion explains spatial continuity.

- Use quick press feedback.
- Use shared transitions for account/card detail.
- Avoid bounce, orbit, pulse, glow, and cinematic movement.

### Containment

Containment groups tasks.

- Root nav contains app sections.
- Payment action opens money movement.
- Dashboard groups around account, action, activity.

## Rejected Misinterpretations

- expressive equals more gradients,
- expressive equals bigger decoration,
- expressive equals playful animation,
- expressive equals Dribbble visual fantasy.
