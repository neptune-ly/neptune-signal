# Neptune Signal Prism Vision

Neptune Signal Prism is the custom fintech design language for Neptune products. It replaces strict Material 3 as the visual target while keeping Material as an implementation and usability guardrail.

Prism should feel dark-first, premium, wallet-grade, Arabic-first, white-label ready, and emotionally polished. It should be visually memorable through controlled gradients, floating surfaces, strong financial hierarchy, and tactile payment moments.

Prism is not a Dribbble clone, crypto skin, glassmorphism kit, neon theme, or decorative layer. It must not trade readability for atmosphere.

Material remains useful for accessibility, touch targets, navigation semantics, color-role discipline, reduced motion, and Compose primitives. Signal owns the actual visual identity.

First components to move into Prism are Home, floating navigation, center payment action, account hero card, wallet actions, quick transfer, financial timeline preview, campaign surfaces, and payment ritual result surfaces.
