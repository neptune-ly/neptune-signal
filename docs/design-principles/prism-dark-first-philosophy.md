# Prism Dark-First Philosophy

Prism Dark is the flagship appearance. It uses deep navy and ink surfaces with controlled cyan, violet, coral, and optional emerald accents.

Dark mode must feel designed, not inverted. It should use atmosphere, depth, and luminous restraint while preserving financial readability.

Prism Light is secondary but still premium: frosted off-white surfaces, soft mist gradients, crisp text, and disciplined accent usage.

Prism OLED is not plain black with borders. It uses near-black surfaces, reduced gradients, lower glow, high contrast, and subtle luminance edges.

Gradients belong to focal surfaces: account hero, payment action, selected campaign surfaces, and payment ritual moments. They do not belong behind small text or every container.
