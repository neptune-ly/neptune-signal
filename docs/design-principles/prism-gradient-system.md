# Prism Gradient System

Prism gradients are controlled financial atmosphere, not decoration.

Allowed gradient roles:
- `prismHeroGradient`: large top or onboarding identity surface.
- `prismAccountGradient`: primary wallet/account hero card.
- `prismPaymentGradient`: center payment action and payment ritual focal object.
- `prismAccentGradient`: small accent surfaces and selected previews.
- `prismSuccessGradient`: success states with financial restraint.
- `prismCampaignGradient`: premium campaign communication.
- `prismAmbientWash`: low-opacity background atmosphere.
- `prismCardGlow`: subtle focal depth only.
- `prismNavSheen`: restrained floating dock highlight.

Rules:
- Text over gradients requires protected contrast.
- Do not stack gradients across adjacent modules.
- Do not use gradients as filler.
- OLED reduces gradient opacity.
- Bank palettes may override gradient endpoints, but not contrast rules.
