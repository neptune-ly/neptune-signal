# Prism White-Label Personalities

Prism supports curated bank personalities instead of unrestricted theming.

Required personalities:
- Classic Bank: restrained, stable, minimal gradients.
- Premium Wallet: default Neptune flagship, dark-first, controlled gradients.
- Youth Fintech: more colorful and animated while readable.
- Corporate Dense: tighter, operational, approvals and treasury ready.
- Islamic Calm: emerald/teal, elegant, quiet.
- Merchant Energy: payment-centric, QR/vPOS friendly, more dynamic actions.

Banks may configure logo, mark, palette, account card gradient, nav style, payment style, typography tone, radius profile, motion profile, density, campaign style, and dark/light/OLED behavior.

Users may choose curated moods and density, but no free RGB picker.
