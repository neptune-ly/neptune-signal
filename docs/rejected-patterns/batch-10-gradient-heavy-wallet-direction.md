# Rejected Pattern: Gradient-Heavy Wallet Direction

Date: 2026-05-15

## Rejected

Gradient-heavy wallet styling is rejected for core banking surfaces.

## Do Not Use

- decorative blobs,
- floating gradient circles,
- full-screen atmospheric gradients,
- glowing surfaces,
- neon fintech color ramps,
- glassmorphism as a product style,
- animated orbit/pulse decoration,
- generic FAB floating over the nav.

## Allowed

- subtle tonal fills,
- semantic color for state,
- one brand splash treatment where appropriate,
- solid primary/secondary action colors,
- restrained shadows in light mode,
- tonal separation in dark/OLED.

## Test Guardrail

Any future gradient must answer:

1. What state or hierarchy does it communicate?
2. Can typography, spacing, or tone do the job instead?
3. Does it survive Arabic, dark mode, and white-label theming?

If the answer is weak, reject it.
