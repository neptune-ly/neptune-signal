# White-Label Theming

Neptune. Signal is white-label ready. Bank colors can change, but Neptune. structure remains.

## Theme Inputs

```text
bank.primary
bank.secondary
bank.accent
bank.ink
```

## Example Theme Families

These are placeholders for presentation and testing, not official brand claims.

| Theme | Primary | Secondary | Accent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neptune. | Navy | Teal | Coral |
| Red Bank | Red | Deep navy | Warm gold |
| Brown Gold Bank | Brown | Gold | Cream |
| Green Bank | Green | Deep green | Sky |
| Blue Bank | Blue | Teal | Coral |

## Rules

- Primary should pass contrast with white.
- Accent should not dominate all surfaces.
- Status colors must remain recognizable even under bank themes.
- Layout and component anatomy must not change per bank.
